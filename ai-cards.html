FAQ

Common questions

How does the NFC work?

Modern iPhones (XR/2018 and newer) and Android phones read NFC automatically. Just tap. Older phones can scan the printed QR code on the back as a fallback.

Can I update my landing page later?

Yes. Your card stays the same — we update the content behind it any time you need. No reprint, no new card.

What can the AI secretary actually do?

It chats with visitors, answers questions about your business, qualifies leads, books meetings to your calendar, and routes hot leads to your inbox in real time. You set the tone, the rules, and the knowledge it draws from.

Why a monthly fee for the AI secretary?

The $5/mo covers ongoing compute, model updates, and quarterly retraining as your business evolves. Cancel any time — your card and landing page keep working.

How long until I receive my cards?

Custom cards ship in 7–10 business days. Metal cards ship in 10–14 business days. AI secretary is activated on delivery.

Are these only for individuals or can I order for my team?

Most clients order for entire sales teams, founder teams, and event staff. Volume pricing available on orders of 25+.
