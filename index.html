How it works

A corporate activation workflow in three moves.

PowerWyze is designed to be simple for guests, practical for event staff, and useful for your revenue team after the booth closes.

  1. Step 1

    Engage

    Guests step up to the kiosk or ambassador experience and choose a branded interaction.

  2. Step 2

    Capture + Enrich

    Lead details, preferences, generated assets, and optional conversation context are collected.

  3. Step 3

    Route + Follow Up

    Structured data is prepared for CRM and sales workflows so teams can act quickly.
