How it works

The revenue workflow, in three moves.

  1. Step 1

    Engage

    Visitor engages through kiosk experience and/or ambassador interaction.

  2. Step 2

    Capture + Enrich

    Wize Pin captures conversation; Wise Lead extracts names, intent, topics, and follow-up timing.

  3. Step 3

    Route + Follow Up

    Structured lead data is handed to CRM and sales workflow for faster conversion.
