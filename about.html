Good questions

A few things
to know.

What can you create for our event?+

Start with a Smart Station and build the experience around your goals: AI photos, brand assistants, games, check-in, lead capture, or live personalized gifting. We’ll confirm the features and logistics in your proposal.

Can we rent or buy a Smart Station?+

Yes. Rent for an event, or buy the hardware with a monthly software subscription. Choose the White Model or Black Model and request a quote for your configuration.

Can everything look like our brand?+

We can tailor the screen interface, content, photo outputs, and station branding to your campaign. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll scope the right setup.

How does lead capture work?+

We plan the information, consent, and delivery flow around your activation. Your proposal will confirm the fields, exports, and any requested integrations.

What do you need from us?+

Your event date, location, audience, goals, and branding are a great starting point. We’ll also confirm power, internet, access, setup, and any production needs.

Where do you operate?+

PowerWyze is based in Miami and works with brands and events across the U.S. Ask us about availability and logistics for your location.
