FAQ

Common questions

What counts as a “qualifying” rental?

Any RoboKiosk rental booking that totals over $1,500 in revenue to PowerWyze — single-day, multi-day, or multi-kiosk. The $200 bonus is paid per booking. Purchases and multi-event contracts have separate payout structures shared after onboarding.

What does “paid to work the event” mean?

When your referral converts, we invite you on-site as part of the activation team. We pay you a day rate (set when the booking is confirmed) to greet attendees, run the kiosk, and represent PowerWyze with the client. You don’t pay for travel, food, or hotel — that’s on us.

How and when do I get paid?

The $200 bonus is paid within 7 business days after the rental clears and the event is delivered. The day rate is paid the week of the event. Payouts via direct deposit, Zelle, or check — your choice.

What if my contact already knows PowerWyze?

We track every lead by referral code and intro source. If you bring us a contact we’re not already actively engaged with, you get credit — full stop. We’ll always confirm attribution before the call.

Do I have to be in Miami / South Florida?

No. PowerWyze deploys nationally. If you have warm intros to brands or agencies anywhere in the U.S., we’ll get the kiosk there. The on-site work just means we cover your travel to whichever city the event lives in.

Is there an exclusive territory?

Top performers move into formal partner status with exclusive territory rights and recurring deal flow. Until then, the program is open — anyone qualified can refer.
